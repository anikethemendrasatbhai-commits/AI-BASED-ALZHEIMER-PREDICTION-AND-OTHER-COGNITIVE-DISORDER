LEARN & UNDERSTAND

Curiosity is a good start.

Learn about cognitive health and understand the difference between practice activities and clinical screening.

01

What is cognitive health?

It includes abilities such as remembering, paying attention, communicating, learning and solving problems.

02

What about clinical tests?

Tools such as MoCA and MMSE are used in appropriate clinical settings. Some instruments have permission or licensing requirements. This demo does not reproduce their official questions.

03

When should I seek help?

If memory or thinking changes persist, worsen, or affect daily life, speak with a qualified healthcare professional. A website activity cannot replace an evaluation.
